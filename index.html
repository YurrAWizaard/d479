          Getting around the island

          • Public buses serve Taniti City daily, 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Private buses reach the rest of the island.
          • Taxis are available throughout Taniti City.
          • Rental cars are available from a local agency beside the airport.
          • Bikes and helmets rent from several vendors — helmets are required by law.
          • On foot: Taniti City is flat and very walkable, and the Merriton Landing area is easy to explore without a car.
